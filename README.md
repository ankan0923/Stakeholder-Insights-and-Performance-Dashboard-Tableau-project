# Stakeholder-Insights-and-Performance-Dashboard
## Summery
This project analyzes insurance stakeholder performance using an interactive Excel/Power BI dashboard. Key findings include total premiums of ₹245.91B, strong value creation by Bancassurance (₹139.91B), and a balanced shareholding distribution led by FII (38.4%). Profitability varies across cities and tenure, with Mumbai and Ahmedabad showing high returns. The dashboard highlights efficiency in cost ratios, stakeholder asset management, and demographic insights, enabling data-driven decisions for insurers and investors.
## Objectives
To visualize premium trends and profitability across cities and tenure.
To evaluate stakeholder contributions (insurers, agents, brokers).
To analyze shareholding distribution and equity creation.
To assess cost ratios and asset management efficiency.
## Methodology
Data Collection: Insurance dataset with premiums, profitability, stakeholders, and demographics.
Dashboard Design: Filters for city, date, stakeholder, profitability, gender, assets, and premium.
Visualization Tools: Bar charts, donut charts, stacked bars, and KPIs.
Analysis Dimensions:
Premium trends (2014–2024),
Stakeholder value creation,
Shareholding distribution,
Profitability by city & tenure,
Cost ratio vs profitability,
Assets under management.
## Analysis & Findings
Premium Trends: Peak values in 2014–2017, stabilizing in later years.
Stakeholder Value: Bancassurance dominates (₹139.91B), followed by partner branches and brokers.
Shareholding: FII (38.4%) and DII (30.2%) hold majority shares, indicating strong institutional involvement.
Profitability by City: Ahmedabad and Mumbai show highest profitability across tenures.
Cost Ratios: Net worth ₹191.2B, with commission ratio ₹22.7B and profit after tax ₹20.4B.
Assets Under Management: People (5,024) lead, followed by distributors and customers.
## Conclusion
The dashboard provides a holistic view of insurance stakeholder performance. Bancassurance and institutional investors drive value creation, while profitability varies significantly across cities. Cost ratios remain manageable, and asset distribution highlights strong engagement from people and distributors.
